07 / INFORMATION CENTER

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

How can I purchase a product?

Browse our catalog, select the product you're interested in, and click the purchase button. You'll be redirected to our official Discord server, where our team will assist you.

What payment methods do you accept?

Available payment methods are provided by our support team during the purchase process. Contact us through Discord for more information.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery times depend on the product and staff availability. Most digital products are processed after payment confirmation. Our team will provide an estimated delivery time before purchase.

Can I get a refund?

Refund eligibility depends on the product, delivery status, and applicable consumer rights. Please review our refund policy or contact support for assistance.

How do I know which product is right for me?

Each product includes a description, pricing information, and relevant requirements. If you're unsure which option suits your needs, our support team can help you compare available products.

Are all products compatible with my PC?

Compatibility varies depending on your hardware, operating system, and product requirements. We recommend confirming compatibility with our team before purchasing.

What is the difference between license options?

License options vary by duration and product. Available plans and their prices are displayed in the product catalog.

Are product statuses updated in real time?

No. Product statuses are maintained manually and may not reflect immediate changes. Contact support to confirm current availability and compatibility.

Will I receive product updates?

Update availability depends on the product and license terms. Important announcements and changes are published through our official Discord server.

Where can I find the latest updates?

Visit the Changelog section of our website or check the announcements channel in our Discord server.

What should I do if my product isn't working?

Open a support ticket and describe the issue. Include your product name, relevant system specifications, and any error messages so our team can assist you.

How long does support usually take?

Response times vary depending on ticket volume and staff availability. Our team handles requests as quickly as possible.

Do I need a Discord account to purchase?

Currently, purchases and customer support are handled through our official Discord server.

How can I verify that I'm speaking with official staff?

Always contact our team through the official Discord server linked on this website. Avoid making payments to unverified accounts.

Is Nocturne Systems affiliated with any game developers?

Nocturne Systems is an independent third-party service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any game developers or publishers.
